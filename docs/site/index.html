PreVisit Desk 是 AI 預問診與慢箋續領檢核助理：用分階段問診整理病史，產生給醫師的摘要；慢性病患者可以先檢核「這次能不能憑慢箋直接領藥」。AI 不診斷、不開藥。PreVisit Desk is an AI pre-visit intake and chronic-prescription refill checker. It collects history in stages and hands the clinician a summary; patients on chronic prescriptions can check whether they can refill directly. The AI never diagnoses or prescribes.

它做什麼What it does

三件事，各有明確的界線。Three jobs, each with a clear boundary.

分階段預問診Staged pre-visit intake

主訴 → 現病史（OPQRST）→ 過去史、用藥、過敏 → 確認摘要。程式追蹤已取得的欄位，缺什麼才問什麼，一次一到兩題。Chief complaint, history of present illness, background, then a confirmed summary. Code tracks the fields; the AI asks only what is missing, one or two questions at a time.

慢箋續領檢核Refill eligibility check

依健保慢性病連續處方箋規則，由 Rust 程式確定性判斷，只回四種結果：可領、尚未到可領期間、需回診、請洽藥師或醫師。Based on Taiwan NHI continuous-prescription rules, decided by deterministic Rust code with exactly four outcomes: eligible, too early, revisit, or consult a pharmacist or doctor.

醫師端待辦Clinician queue

待審摘要、完整對話與工具追蹤、轉交單；紅旗與需要人工處理的案件排在最前面。Pending summaries, the full conversation with its tool trace, and handoff tickets; red-flag and human-needed cases come first.

預定用途與責任界線Intended use and responsibility

不同用途需要不同的證據與責任。本產品只承擔「資訊整理」與「規則計算」。Different uses need different evidence and accountability. This product only takes on information gathering and rule calculation.

用途Use本產品的立場Our position
診療、診斷、開藥Treatment, diagnosis, prescribing不做。有人要求時，固定回覆拒絕，並轉給醫師。Not done. Requests get a fixed refusal and a handoff to a clinician.
分流Triage只做紅旗規則：命中就固定導向 119／急診，不經模型。Red-flag rules only: a hit gives fixed 119 / emergency guidance without calling the model.
行政Administration做：預問診摘要、慢箋可領日期檢核。Yes: pre-visit summaries and refill date checks.
衛教Patient education只引用官方來源手冊回答流程與就醫時機，不給劑量，每段附來源與查證日。Quotes an official-source handbook for process and when to seek care; no doses; every passage carries its source and check date.
心理支持Emotional support不做。偵測到自傷念頭就固定轉 1925 安心專線與 119。Not done. Self-harm signals get a fixed pointer to the 1925 hotline and 119.

六條安全界線Six safety boundaries

每一條都有對應的程式位置，也都在評測題庫裡逐條驗證。Each has a place in the code and is checked case by case in the eval set.

1開場揭露Disclosure first

「我是 AI 預問診助理，不是醫師。」第一則固定訊息，頁面上方也一直有橫幅。“I am an AI pre-visit assistant, not a doctor.” A fixed first message, plus a permanent banner.

2紅旗先於模型Red flags before the model

胸痛、呼吸困難、單側無力、意識不清、吐血、嚴重過敏、自傷念頭等，命中就固定回覆，不呼叫模型。Chest pain, breathing trouble, one-sided weakness, collapse, vomiting blood, severe allergy, self-harm: a hit returns fixed text and never calls the model.

3不診斷、不開藥、不給劑量No diagnosis, no prescribing, no doses

提示詞、越界規則與輸出後檢查三層；違反就改寫或拒絕。Three layers: the prompt, scope rules, and an output check that rewrites or refuses.

4慢箋只回四種結果Four refill outcomes only

管制藥、症狀變化、副作用一律轉人，並建立轉交單。Controlled drugs, changed symptoms and side effects always go to a human with a ticket.

5可稽核Auditable

每次工具呼叫、紅旗結果、模型名稱與用量都進追蹤面板與資料庫。Every tool call, red-flag result, model name and token count lands in the trace panel and the database.

6隱私Privacy

不設姓名與身分證欄位，疑似個資在存檔與送模型前遮蔽，資料到期整筆刪除。No name or ID fields; likely identifiers are masked before storage and before any model call; visits are deleted after the retention period.

畫面Screens

手機與桌面都能用；淺色與深色都符合 WCAG AA 對比。Works on phones and desktops; light and dark themes both meet WCAG AA contrast.

限制Limits

這是課堂展示專案，不是醫療器材，也不是醫療建議。請勿輸入真實病歷或個資；免費層的模型呼叫內容，模型供應商可能拿去改善產品。公開展示站有速率限制與每日呼叫上限，額度用完時紅旗規則與慢箋檢核仍可使用。This is a course demo, not a medical device and not medical advice. Do not enter real records or personal data; free-tier model traffic may be used by the provider to improve its products. The public demo has rate limits and a daily call cap; red-flag rules and the refill check keep working when the cap is reached.
